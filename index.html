Follow the idea
where it goes.

Start with a MIDI file, a note, or a phrase you sing. Accolai gives each idea room to become a song.

First, catch a phrase.

Sing or hum a melody and turn a take into notes you can edit. Or open a MIDI file and pick up where you left off.

Then, shape the notes.

Move through the piano roll, adjust timing and velocity, and make the musical details feel like yours.

Now, build the song.

Arrange parts across tracks, listen back, and keep refining without leaving your creative flow.

Every note
in its place.
